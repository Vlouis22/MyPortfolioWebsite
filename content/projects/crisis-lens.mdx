## Overview

CrisisLens was built to make breaking news more useful in the moment. Instead of stopping at headlines, the product turns a fast-moving crisis into something people can understand, track, and respond to in real time.

![CrisisLens product interface](/images/projects/crisis-lens/CrisisLens.png)

## What we built

We combined an interactive globe, AI-generated summaries, impact analysis, and "what to watch next" guidance into one live experience. The product also included a personalized For You feed, multilingual summaries, and an in-app donation flow so users could support relief efforts without leaving the app.

<Callout title="What made it strong">
The most important part was that it felt like a real product, not just a weekend prototype. Every feature was meant to help someone understand a crisis and take the next useful step.
</Callout>

## Results

We brought together Firebase, NewsAPI.ai, OpenAI, Google Maps geocoding, and Stripe into a product that connected awareness with action. It was one of those builds that proved product thinking matters just as much as shipping speed.
