## Overview

I built the official HEIM Church website to give the church a stronger online presence and a better experience for both regular members and first time visitors.

![HEIM Church website homepage](/images/projects/heim-church/heim-church-site.png)

## What I built

The site includes multilingual support, live streaming access, and secure payments. I focused on responsiveness and made sure the experience worked well across phones, tablets, and desktops.

<Callout title="Focus">
My goal was to make the site feel welcoming, easy to navigate, and useful for each and everyone at the church.
</Callout>

## Results

The website supports 100+ weekly users and helped improve engagement and accessibility. I also optimized performance and reduced load times by 40 percent across devices.
