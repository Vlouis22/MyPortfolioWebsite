## Overview

TopTier Strategy Mobile was created to make serious investing workflows feel practical on a phone. Many investors want to research ideas, review portfolios, and monitor performance throughout the day, but most mobile experiences either feel too shallow or too cluttered to be genuinely useful.

I built the mobile app to extend the core TopTier Strategy experience into a focused product that feels clear, responsive, and easy to navigate. Users can research a company quickly, review portfolio ideas, track watchlists and performance, and stay connected to their strategy without relying on a desktop workflow.

![TopTier Strategy mobile app concept](/images/projects/toptier-strategy-mobile/app-store-preview.png)

## What I built

I built the app in React Native with Expo and Expo Router, connecting it to the same backend platform that powers the web product. The app includes structured research reports with business context, financial statements, ratios, profitability trends, growth trends, and clear bull and bear case summaries. It also includes portfolio builder flows, watchlist tools, performance dashboards, synced user data, notifications, and premium subscription support.

From an engineering perspective, I implemented secure authentication, local persistence, data synchronization, Apple sign in, in app purchases, and a polished mobile interface optimized for fast repeat use. The initial launch focused on iOS, while the technical foundation supports a broader mobile footprint as the product grows.

<Callout title="Product focus">
I wanted the mobile experience to feel fast, trustworthy, and confidence building. The goal was to help users understand a company or check on their portfolio in moments that matter, without sacrificing depth, structure, or clarity.
</Callout>

## Results

The mobile app proved that TopTier Strategy could extend beyond the web into a strong mobile experience without losing the quality of the underlying product. It highlights my ability to translate a complex platform into a focused mobile application that feels polished, useful, and ready for real world use.
