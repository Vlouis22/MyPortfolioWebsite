## Overview

Investing still feels harder than it should for most people. Valuable information is spread across screeners, earnings reports, portfolio tools, and brokerage platforms, which makes the process slow, fragmented, and intimidating for many individual investors.

I built TopTier Strategy to turn that experience into a clear product workflow. The web platform brings stock discovery, structured company research, portfolio construction, brokerage connected portfolio tracking, and long term performance monitoring into one system so users can move from an idea to a decision with far more clarity and confidence.

![TopTier Strategy web platform concept](/images/projects/toptier-strategy-web/web-platform-preview.png)

## What I built

I designed and built the platform across the full stack. At the core is a five pillar research framework that evaluates a company across valuation, profitability, financial health, shareholder returns, and growth outlook. It transforms raw financial data into a structured research experience with clear scoring, financial statements, trend analysis, and concise bull and bear case summaries.

I also built a portfolio builder that uses hedge fund holdings and user preferences to generate tailored portfolio ideas, along with dashboards for allocations, watchlists, projections, performance, and daily movement. To support the broader product, I integrated secure authentication, subscription billing, brokerage connectivity, background jobs, and shared data services so the experience feels cohesive from onboarding through ongoing portfolio management.

On the technical side, the platform combines a React frontend with a Python and Flask backend, Firebase authentication, Firestore and Supabase data services, Redis backed infrastructure, Stripe billing, SnapTrade brokerage syncing, and AI assisted research workflows.

<Callout title="Product focus">
My goal was to make high quality investment research feel structured, approachable, and actionable. Every part of the product was designed to reduce cognitive load and help users understand what matters without jumping between disconnected tools.
</Callout>
