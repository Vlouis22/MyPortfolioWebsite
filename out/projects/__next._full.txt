CrisisLens was built to make breaking news more useful in the moment. Instead of stopping at headlines, the product turns a fast-moving crisis into something people can understand, track, and respond to in real time.

## Overview

Investing still feels harder than it should for most people. Valuable information is spread across screeners, earnings reports, portfolio tools, and brokerage platforms, which makes the process slow, fragmented, and intimidating for many individual investors.

I built TopTier Strategy to turn that experience into a clear product workflow. The web platform brings stock discovery, structured company research, portfolio construction, brokerage connected portfolio tracking, and long term performance monitoring into one system so users can move from an idea to a decision with far more clarity and confidence.

![TopTier Strategy web platform concept](/images/projects/toptier-strategy-web/web-platform-preview.png)

## What I built

I designed and built the platform across the full stack. At the core is a five pillar research framework that evaluates a company across valuation, profitability, financial health, shareholder returns, and growth outlook. It transforms raw financial data into a structured research experience with clear scoring, financial statements, trend analysis, and concise bull and bear case summaries.

I also built a portfolio builder that uses hedge fund holdings and user preferences to generate tailored portfolio ideas, along with dashboards for allocations, watchlists, projections, performance, and daily movement. To support the broader product, I integrated secure authentication, subscription billing, brokerage connectivity, background jobs, and shared data services so the experience feels cohesive from onboarding through ongoing portfolio management.

On the technical side, the platform combines a React frontend with a Python and Flask backend, Firebase authentication, Firestore and Supabase data services, Redis backed infrastructure, Stripe billing, SnapTrade brokerage syncing, and AI assisted research workflows.

<Callout title="Product focus">
My goal was to make high quality investment research feel structured, approachable, and actionable. Every part of the product was designed to reduce cognitive load and help users understand what matters without jumping between disconnected tools.
</Callout>

## Results

TopTier Strategy demonstrates my ability to take a complex financial workflow from concept to production and shape both the product strategy and the engineering behind it. It is a strong example of founder led product development, full stack execution, and thoughtful user experience design in a high trust domain.

## Overview

TopTier Strategy Mobile was created to make serious investing workflows feel practical on a phone. Many investors want to research ideas, review portfolios, and monitor performance throughout the day, but most mobile experiences either feel too shallow or too cluttered to be genuinely useful.

I built the mobile app to extend the core TopTier Strategy experience into a focused product that feels clear, responsive, and easy to navigate. Users can research a company quickly, review portfolio ideas, track watchlists and performance, and stay connected to their strategy without relying on a desktop workflow.

![TopTier Strategy mobile app concept](/images/projects/toptier-strategy-mobile/app-store-preview.png)

## What I built

I built the app in React Native with Expo and Expo Router, connecting it to the same backend platform that powers the web product. The app includes structured research reports with business context, financial statements, ratios, profitability trends, growth trends, and clear bull and bear case summaries. It also includes portfolio builder flows, watchlist tools, performance dashboards, synced user data, notifications, and premium subscription support.

From an engineering perspective, I implemented secure authentication, local persistence, data synchronization, Apple sign in, in app purchases, and a polished mobile interface optimized for fast repeat use. The initial launch focused on iOS, while the technical foundation supports a broader mobile footprint as the product grows.

<Callout title="Product focus">
I wanted the mobile experience to feel fast, trustworthy, and confidence building. The goal was to help users understand a company or check on their portfolio in moments that matter, without sacrificing depth, structure, or clarity.
</Callout>

## Results

The mobile app proved that TopTier Strategy could extend beyond the web into a strong mobile experience without losing the quality of the underlying product. It highlights my ability to translate a complex platform into a focused mobile application that feels polished, useful, and ready for real world use.

## Overview

Dr. House is a research project focused on building an AI assisted clinical reasoning system that helps organize and interpret multiple forms of patient data in one workflow. Instead of treating symptoms, notes, lab results, medical images, and patient history as isolated inputs, the project brings them together into a single structured patient profile that can support faster and more informed clinical decision making.

My work focused on the backend systems that make this possible. I built the pipeline that ingests clinical text, patient audio, health records, and laboratory data, processes each source with task specific models, and converts the results into a consistent structured profile that downstream reasoning components can use.

![Dr. House architecture concept](/images/projects/clinical-text-agent/thumb.svg)

## What I built

I developed a modular backend architecture for multimodal clinical intake and information extraction. The system supports transcription for patient voice input, medical entity extraction from notes and records, summarization of clinical context, lab report parsing, normalization of results, abnormality detection, and schema based output generation. I also designed the fusion layer that combines information from different sources into a unified patient profile, creating the foundation for reasoning and explainable diagnosis in the larger Dr. House system.

<Callout title="Why it mattered">
What made this project valuable was that it went beyond calling models in isolation. The real challenge was designing a reliable backend that could coordinate different AI components, handle diverse medical inputs, preserve clinical structure, and produce outputs that were useful for a reasoning agent rather than just technically correct on their own.
</Callout>

## Results

This project deepened my experience in applied AI backend engineering, multimodal systems design, structured data pipelines, and clinical information modeling. It represents my contribution to a broader vision for AI assisted healthcare tools that can help clinicians review complex patient information more efficiently and understand why certain diagnostic suggestions are being made.
