Overview

Hornet Helper was built to help students find study partners, join open sessions, and get support more easily. I led a team of 3 and helped shape both the direction of the product and the final implementation.

What I built

I built the front end in React so students could browse and join study sessions in a simple way. We also added real time communication and an AI powered assistant that could help match students with study partners and provide on demand academic support.

Why it mattered

Hornet Helper solved a real problem by making it fast and easy for students to find study support. By focusing on simplicity and speed, we created an experience students could use immediately, showing how the right features can drive real impact.

Results

The project won 1st place in a 15 team hackathon. More importantly, it showed how a focused product with the right features can create immediate value for students.
